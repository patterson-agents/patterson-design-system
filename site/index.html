Foundations

Navy and sky on generous white.

Navy carries structure, sky carries accent, and white does most of the work. The tertiary set is reserved for charts — it never becomes page chrome.

Bold solutions and a personal touch.body · 400

Only 400 and 700 are licensed faces. The theme asks for 800 on h1/h2, so production renders synthesized bold.

Shape — restrained, not round

field · 2px
button · 5px
card · 10px

The production stylesheet contains no pill radius anywhere. Buttons are 5px and fields are 2px — the pill token exists, but it is a house convention rather than brand truth.

Verified, not inferred

Twelve things everyone gets wrong.

This system was checked against the production theme stylesheet, the licensed Typekit kit and the real icon font. Several intuitive-seeming rules turned out to be false — they are corrected here and tagged in the spec.

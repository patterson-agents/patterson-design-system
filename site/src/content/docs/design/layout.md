## Spacing system

- **Base unit:** 4px.
- **Tokens:** 1 → 4px · 2 → 8px · 3 → 12px · 4 → 16px · 5 → 24px · 6 → 32px · 7 → 40px ·
  8 → 48px · 9 → 64px · 10 → 80px · 11 → 96px · 12 → 128px. See the
  [spacing token page](/tokens/spacing/) for the generated values.
- **Section padding:** 64–128px vertical; 96px is the default. Hero and closing bands
  go to 128px.
- **Card internal padding:** 32px standard, 24px for compact and storefront-density
  cards.
- **Stack rhythm inside a band:** eyebrow → 12px → head → 16px → lead → 32px → content.

## Grid & container

- **Max content width:** 1240px, centered, with a fluid gutter of 20 → 64px.
- **Readable measure:** 720px for article and documentation body copy.
- **Hero:** 6/6 or 7/5 split — headline + lead + button row left, photography right (or
  full-bleed photo with navy scrim).
- **Card grids:** 3-up desktop / 2-up tablet / 1-up mobile. Stat rows 4-up desktop /
  2-up mobile.
- **Storefront product grids:** 4-up or 5-up desktop — higher density than corporate
  surfaces, and the one place density beats whitespace.

## Whitespace philosophy

**White space is part of the brand.** Generous, uncramped, never dense on corporate and
marketing surfaces — the calm is the message. Product and storefront surfaces are
permitted to tighten (they carry catalogs and tables), but corporate pages, decks, and
documentation stay roomy.

See [Responsive behavior](/design/appendix/#responsive-behavior) for how this grid
collapses across breakpoints.
